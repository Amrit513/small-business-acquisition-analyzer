# Publishing the portfolio preview

Acquisition Lens lets someone explore a small business purchase under assumptions they choose. A math test can establish that an example computes correctly; it cannot establish that a seller told the truth or that a deal is a good purchase. This US-focused editorial and engineering checklist is not a legal opinion, liability waiver, or guarantee against claims.

## Before sharing a release

Record the commit, date, artifact tested and result in the [release ledger](REDESIGN-RELEASE.md). Leave untested items pending.

- **Check the claim where readers see it.** Review one sample from input to comparison to exported PDF. Label it fictional in shared artifacts. Describe cash flow as pretax if taxes are excluded; disclose any omitted buyer labor, capital spending or working capital beside the number. A coverage threshold is a screening assumption, not lender approval. An editable valuation range is not an appraisal. Do not turn unknowns or a funding gap into a favorable verdict. Correct a misleading headline rather than relying on a footer. See the [FTC’s advertising FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) and [digital disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/plain-language/bus41-dot-com-disclosures-information-about-online-advertising.pdf).
- **Test what leaves the device.** Use a distinctive fictional business name and amount. Inspect requests while capturing, editing, saving, restoring, comparing and exporting on the built app and extension. Verify neither request URLs nor bodies carry the deal; check production logging and analytics settings before making claims about those services. Explain that an ordinary page request still reaches the host. A transfer fragment can be read by the destination page and browser; an exported file can disclose excerpts to anyone who receives it. Test deletion and reimport using fictional data. Device storage is neither cross-device sync nor a guaranteed backup.
- **Clear the materials actually shipped.** Use invented listings and original or permissioned visuals in demos, screenshots and downloadable examples. For every copied component, font, icon or bundled dependency, record version/source, license and required notices. Inspect the final ZIP and web distribution for the notices they require, including the project [MIT license](../LICENSE). A public repository is not by itself permission to redistribute someone else’s code; see [GitHub’s licensing guide](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).
- **Be specific about authorship.** Credit AI-assisted development and identify the creator’s actual design, revision and testing decisions. Under the [US Copyright Office’s January 29, 2025 report summary](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html), human-authored expression may be protected in an assisted work; prompts alone are not enough. This does not clear third-party rights.

## When to pause for a human decision

Keep cloud AI disabled. [Gemini’s API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) require adult API users and restrict clients directed at or likely to be accessed by people under 18. An adult’s account does not remove that audience restriction. For any future hosting, store or commercial agreement, check the specific terms; have an eligible, authorized operator accept obligations only where permitted.

Before offering paid personalized securities recommendations, earning transaction-based referral fees, or holding confidential deal documents for others, have a lawyer familiar with the operator’s jurisdiction review the proposed activity and data handling. The [SEC’s explanation of investment advisers](https://www.sec.gov/files/investor/pubs/invadvisers.htm) concerns the business of advising on securities; whether a particular business acquisition or service falls within a regulatory regime is fact-specific. An “educational” label does not decide that question.
